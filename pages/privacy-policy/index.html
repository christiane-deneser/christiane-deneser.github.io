Cookies and tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising, or other tracking technologies. No user profiles are created and no information about your browsing behaviour is collected for tracking or advertising purposes. The website does use Adobe Fonts, as described above. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to request access to your personal data, correction of inaccurate data, deletion of your data, restriction of processing, and, where applicable, data portability. Where processing is based on your consent, you have the right to withdraw your consent at any time. The withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal. Where processing is based on legitimate interests, you have the right to object to the processing on grounds relating to your particular situation. To exercise your rights, please contact me using the email address provided above.

Right to lodge a complaint

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU Member State of your habitual residence, place of work, or place of the alleged infringement.

SSL/TLS encryption

This website uses HTTPS and TLS encryption to protect data transmitted between your browser and the website. This helps protect information against interception during transmission.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to this website, the services I use, or applicable legal requirements. The current version of this Privacy Policy will always be published on this website. The date of the latest update is shown at the top of this page.
