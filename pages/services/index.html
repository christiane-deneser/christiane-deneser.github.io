Accessibility Strategy & Consulting

I help you understand your current accessibility maturity, identify your goals, and define a practical strategy that fits your product, team, and ways of working. Together, we’ll prioritise the next steps, establish a realistic roadmap, and build a sustainable approach to accessibility.

Typical services include

  • Needs assessments and getting started with accessibility improvements
  • Accessibility prioritisation and implementation planning
  • Accessibility governance, roles, and responsibilities
  • European Accessibility Act readiness

Accessibility Audits

As an accredited BIK BITV-Test-Prüfstelle, I conduct accessibility audits using the official BIK BITV-Test and BIK WCAG-Test methodologies. Through an objective assessment, I help you identify barriers and evaluate the current accessibility status of your web products. You will receive a comprehensive audit report with detailed findings and clear remediation recommendations to help you navigate your roadmap towards conformance with recognised accessibility standards. In addition to complete website audits, I also offer focused assessments for smaller scopes, such as individual user journeys, design systems, components, or newly developed features.

Typical services include

  • Objective accessibility assessments for agencies, businesses, and public sector organisations
  • BIK BITV-Test and BIK WCAG-Test for websites and web applications
  • Thorough manual testing against recognised standards, including EN 301 549 and WCAG 2.2
  • Executive reports with clear findings, practical guidance, and remediation recommendations

Accessibility Engineering & Coaching

I provide hands-on accessibility implementation and remediation. Hands-on work is always combined with knowledge transfer, so accessibility skills stay in your team long after my engagement ends. Since accessibility works best when teams understand how to implement it themselves, I provide coaching and mentoring for your UX/UI designers, developers, and content and copy teams.

Typical services include

  • Frontend accessibility implementation and remediation
  • Code, component, and design reviews, feedback, and practical examples tailored to the product
  • Training and workshops for cross-functional product teams
  • Establishing shared accessibility responsibilities, and ownership

Long-term Integration & Accessibility Programs

I help your team move from one-off fixes to long-term accessibility maturity. I work with your team to introduce accessibility early and consistently into the product lifecycle and everyday workflows, rather than treating it as a final compliance step.

Typical services include

  • Integrating accessibility into discovery, requirements, and acceptance criteria
  • Aligning accessibility practices with Scrum and agile workflows
  • Supporting product, engineering, design, and content stakeholders
  • Establishing or improving accessibility programs across the organisation
