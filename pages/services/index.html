Accessibility Audits

As an accredited BIK BITV-Test-Prüfstelle, I conduct accessibility audits using the official BIK BITV-Test and BIK WCAG-Test methodologies. Through an objective assessment, I help you measure and evaluate the accessibility of your web products. You will receive a comprehensive audit report with detailed findings and clear remediation recommendations to help you achieve conformance with recognised accessibility standards. In addition to complete website audits, I also offer focused assessments for smaller scopes, such as individual user journeys, design systems, components, or newly developed features.

Typical services include

  • Objective accessibility assessments for agencies, businesses, and public sector organisations
  • BIK BITV-Test and BIK WCAG-Test for websites and web applications
  • Thorough manual testing against recognised standards, including EN 301 549 and WCAG 2.2
  • Executive reports with clear findings, practical guidance, and remediation recommendations

Accessibility Engineering & Enablement

My background in frontend engineering and agile software development allows me to support and enable your product teams at different levels. Through embedded accessibility support, I provide hands-on accessibility implementation and remediation. I work directly with developers, designers, and content teams to improve accessibility throughout the product lifecycle while building lasting in-house accessibility expertise within your organisation.

Typical services

  • Frontend accessibility implementation and remediation
  • Code, component, and design reviews
  • Knowledge transfer through coaching, mentoring, training, and workshops for cross-functional product teams
  • Establishing accessibility roles, responsibilities, and ownership
