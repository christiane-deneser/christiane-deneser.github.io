Accessibility Consulting

Embed accessibility into your product lifecycle from the beginning. I help product teams integrate accessibility into agile workflows, product strategy, and design and development processes, creating a strong foundation for accessible digital products.

Typical services

  • Accessibility strategy
  • Product lifecycle integration
  • Agile & Scrum consulting
  • Accessibility roadmaps
  • European Accessibility Act readiness

Accessibility Audits

Gain an independent assessment of your website or application using the official BIK BITV-Test and BIK WCAG-Test methodologies. Receive clear findings, prioritised remediation recommendations, and practical guidance for achieving accessibility conformance.

Typical services

  • BIK BITV-Test
  • BIK WCAG-Test
  • EN 301 549 & WCAG 2.2 evaluation
  • Baseline assessments
  • Remediation planning

My Collaboration Process

1 Introductory Call

We'll discuss your product, your goals, your current challenges, and whether I'm the right person to help.

2 Proposal

Based on our conversation, I'll prepare a tailored proposal outlining: recommended services project scope estimated timeline pricing expected deliverables

3 Agreement

Once we agree on the scope, we'll confirm the collaboration in writing. Depending on your procurement process, we can work with your contract or a consulting agreement that I provide.

4 Delivery

We'll work together using the collaboration model that best suits your team—whether that's an audit, a fixed-term project, or ongoing support. Regular communication and transparency are important throughout the engagement.

5 Next Steps

At the end of the engagement, you'll receive the agreed deliverables and, if appropriate, recommendations for future improvements or continued support.
