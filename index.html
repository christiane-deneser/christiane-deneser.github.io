Hi, I’m Christiane

I’m an accessibility expert and frontend developer working at the intersection of digital accessibility, product development, and agile processes.

Over the past ten years, I’ve built accessible frontends, collaborated with international product teams, and led accessibility program work at Ableton. Today, I help organisations build more accessible digital products through independent audits, strategic consulting, hands-on implementation, and long-term accessibility enablement. As an accredited BIK BITV-Test-Prüfstelle, I conduct accessibility audits using the official BIK BITV-Testand BIK WCAG-Test methodologies.

If you’re looking for a collaborative accessibility partner, I’d love to hear about your project.
